# Scope

[06. rules](06.%20rules.md) holds the decisions and is what a run reads. This file holds why each one exists, because a rule whose reason is lost gets argued away.

Every entry below is a mistake that reached him. The dates are dropped, since the rule is current either way, and the reasoning is kept, since that is the part that cannot be reconstructed.

# How these failures repeat

Three patterns produced most of the entries, and recognising them is worth more than any single rule.

1. **Truth is necessary and not sufficient.** A six-agent critic fleet passed ten letters that each broke the same rules, because every sentence in them was true. A document can be factually unimpeachable and still fail its job.
2. **A rule an agent must choose to follow will eventually not be followed.** When a whole batch breaks a rule rather than one document, prose in a skill file is not the fix. The check moves into code, failure blocks delivery, and the check runs over the existing archive to find out how long it has been broken.
3. **Fixing one surface is not fixing the rule.** The punctuation ban was corrected three times, once for bullets, once for letters, once for emails. A document rule lives in the compiled PDF, so a sweep that edits sources and does not recompile leaves the artifact a recruiter opens still broken.

# Claims and evidence

A CV claimed "German A2, actively improving, pursuing B1" when the record held A2 only. Language levels are stated exactly as evidenced, with no aspirational qualifier.

A CV listed C++17 where the record says plain C++. He later confirmed the Continental work was C++14 and that he is learning C++17, and ruled that the version ban stays anyway. That fact exists for scoring and interview preparation, never for a document.

A CV said the segmentation pipeline was "for image-guided catheter navigation", promoting a downstream use into the headline purpose to match a keyword. The work is phantom fabrication. Map the chain of what he did, what it produced and what that enables, and place the term where it truly sits. The test is whether an interviewer opening with "so you're the one who does X" can be answered yes without qualification.

The same document invented "risk-aware verification", a practice that does not exist, and added the clause "rigor directly transferable to medical device software". An invented activity name is an invented fact, and a bullet never argues its own relevance.

A CV relabelled "Linux-based Jetson AGX Orin" as "Embedded Linux". The Orin runs full L4T and Ubuntu with no Yocto, no BSP and no cross-compilation behind it. A device label never upgrades into a discipline claim.

A nightly critic challenged "structured scene graphs" as unevidenced. The thesis sources carry a `scene_graph.py` module and the term appears 69 times, so the gap was in the note rather than in the work. The correct response to a challenged sentence is to re-read the source and show the line. Defending it from memory and withdrawing it reflexively are both failures.

A CV carried "fixed sampler seed" for the thesis determinism. The thesis states the opposite, that no seed is passed and the library defaults it to zero. He measured byte-identical output and traced the cause, which is the stronger claim anyway. Diagnosing why a system is deterministic beats claiming to have configured it.

A bullet said the maze stack was being extended with Nav2 and an LLM navigator, traced to a status he had stated. His own design notes two days later rejected that approach for that environment, and the active design is map-based re-localisation with deferred loop closure. A repository outranks a dated statement, and the accurate bullet was the stronger one.

He confirmed RS485 and Modbus are absent, as are PLC and TwinCAT, DO-178C and DO-254, network pruning, camera calibration, low-power firmware, GNSS, cellular and STM32. Industrial fieldbus as a family is absent, so a fieldbus requirement is a gap and the protocols are not asked about one at a time.

# Matching and triage

Eight roles were shortlisted from list-pane metadata alone, with no description opened. Reading them afterwards killed or flagged every one, through a fluent Spanish requirement, a misattributed agency advert, a Dutch requirement, a wrong discipline, and three that breached the freshness window because the board's refresh date hid the real posting date. A keyword sweep is never presented as a list of matches.

A requirement was scored as a gap by inferring that his professional work was algorithm-layer only. He has worked at hardware level professionally, and the evidence was already on file. Never score a requirement as a gap on inference when the evidence is ambiguous. Ask. The question costs one message and the wrong score costs a job. Scoring is neither low-balled by inventing gaps nor hyped by inflating fit.

A defence posting required the nationality of a NATO member or close ally, which his documented citizenship fails, despite a 4.3 technical fit. A stated requirement plus a documented fact is a legitimate skip with no speculation. Where the requirement is unstated it is an early question and not a blocker. The wider consequence is the deprioritisation, since these bars surface late and waste the effort already spent.

The first scan surfaced unrelated noise through substring matching with no location filter. Filters use word boundaries and location allowlists.

The tracker filled with recirculated postings wearing fresh dates. A posting labelled as reposted is dropped, and an id sitting far below the sweep's median while carrying a fresh date is treated the same way.

A role older than the freshness window does not enter, including a hand-pasted URL and including a newly added company's standing postings. Exceptional fit does not override freshness.

# Documents

The first generation batch invented a summary section, invented a thesis showcase, broke reverse chronology and rewrote the tagline. The structure is frozen. Only the two sanctioned slots vary.

Two distinct Valeo bullets, the Hidden Markov model and the data pipeline, were merged to reduce machine learning emphasis for a firmware posting. That under-represents real substance to project a posting-shaped image, which is keyword matching inverted. Emphasis comes from ordering. Merging is only for genuine redundancy.

A validation bullet's head verb was replaced with the posting's responsibility language, turning "Validated software" into "Debugged complex multi-variable system issues", which is incoherent because software-in-the-loop benchmarking is not a debugging technique. The honest match was already in the bullet's method clause and only needed reordering.

Told to remove tailoring violations, the next pass reverted whole CVs to baseline, which erases the point of a tailored document. Violations are fixed surgically while legitimate tailoring is kept. The goal is variants that differ by the order and framing of true work, not clones and not keyword-stuffed documents.

A project was titled by its goal state rather than its current state. He later chose to keep the goal-state title together with an explicit ongoing marker and an extending bullet, so that pattern is settled and is not re-flagged.

An in-progress project entered a CV on a verbal statement alone. A project needs a repo-verified note marked `cv-ready: true` before it grounds a bullet.

The courses section was removed and the space given to projects, and a qualifying course returns as a single line inside Academics and never as its own heading. The measured consequence is that a line does not displace a project where a section did, so the page count is measured before anything is removed.

Dropping `\photo{}` does not remove the picture, because the class draws an empty circle labelled Photo instead. `\nophoto` removes the image, the circle and the reserved column. A side effect worth knowing is that the portrait was most of the file weight, so a photo-free build is tens of kilobytes rather than hundreds.

An award parenthetical in a project title stated the same fact twice, once in a slot reserved for identity, and the second statement is the one a reader discounts.

The CV said the degree ended in June while every letter said enrolment runs to the end of September. Both were true and four critics read the packet as contradicting itself on the front page, so the line reads September.

Two authority files disagreed on artifact naming, one requiring a date and one not, and the run followed the one without it. A fortnight of documents shipped undated and nothing checked the name. Where two files can disagree, one is named the source of truth and the other points at it.

A sweep corrected the sources and recompiled only part of the batch, so three PDFs still carried the banned string while every source file passed. Verification reads the compiled output, never the sources just edited.

# Letters

Six letters took the first public fact found and hyped it as motivation. Any fact that survives a company swap is banned. Research verifies and sharpens a motivation derived from his own files, and it never sources one.

Nine of ten letters in one batch never said what the company builds, because the research step was skipped. With no tie to the actual work, each letter fell back on the two things always available without research, which are reciting the CV and volunteering gaps. Across the whole archive, 43 of 83 letters failed the same way.

A letter disclosed gaps in a whole paragraph, including an offer to learn hardware quickly. Gaps are interview material.

A letter claimed DO-178C competence by transfer from automotive process work. A serious reader hears that as cocky and ignorant.

A letter was addressed to the contact the advert named for questions, who is not the application's reader. A wrong name at the top reads worse than a neutral greeting.

A letter said "a testbed whose software I wrote end to end", which reads as a wannabe. Authorship shows through concrete first person verbs. Totality framing, meaning end to end, entire, myself as an intensifier and single handedly, comes out while the claim stays direct.

A letter said "university research that asked X", letting one project impersonate a degree. Scope words match the record in both directions.

A letter said "control has been the through line of my work", which is a grand-arc self-portrait. The fix is the plain factual sequence.

A closer said "producing that kind of number is the engineering I want more of", and when banned it returned twice in plainer wording. The ban is the tacked-on want or sentiment sentence itself, not its polish.

A transition read "that project turned the question into a number", which was rule-compliant and still failed, because the compression itself is the tell. When a sentence gets clever to satisfy the rules, uncleverness wins.

A letter opened on a formula, and every letter opened on the same tell. Stock openers are banned as a class.

A paragraph became a benchmark datasheet of bandwidth figures, clock rates and token rates instead of a story of curiosity and craft. The number budget is four.

A closing paragraph recited a comma-separated inventory of skills followed by HR boilerplate. It is replaced by the first concrete task he would take up, why the preceding result equips him for it, availability, and a collegial close.

A draft wrote "the work convinced me that a sensing budget is best spent on the states hardest to estimate", stretching a views entry outside its stated scope and deriving a belief from a work fact. His verdict was that it is not his to own. An entry outside its scope does not exist for that letter.

A linker read "my studies supplied the control side", which is a section header in prose clothing. Delete it, and if no fact is lost it was a signpost.

A letter recounted the ball balancer in full with controller, task periods and trial results, answering nothing the letter had raised. An experience enters because the letter raised something it answers, and closes on what it established.

A draft fused a production module, a prototype and a trainee phase into one continuous work. Fusion is misrepresentation by narrative.

A draft restated the advert's own requirements back to the reader. The reader wrote the advert, so quoting their ask next to his answer is the emptiest sentence a letter can carry.

A draft told the same fact twice in one paragraph in different words. Two facts told four times is a failed letter rather than a thorough one.

A draft passed every ban and carried no motivation at all. A fit claim says he can, not why he wants. Point at the sentence carrying the why, or the letter has failed its only job.

A short-form application box was filled with two full CV bullets including metrics. The resume carries the evidence and the box carries only what the resume cannot say, which is the through-line, the want, the forward intent and logistics.

A letter framed a project as a side project done on his own time. Letters speak as a working engineer, never as a student or hobbyist.

The recurring root cause behind the inventory fallback is that some notes hold bullets rather than decisions and difficulties. The standing fix is a depth interview per experience, not a better prompt.

## 2026-10-15, the MOTOR Ai letter, the first one he accepted

The letter took eleven rounds of his corrections. Each lesson below is what one round cost, and the rules in 06. rules Letters were rewritten from them.

A run skipped reading the talk-to-me and human-voice files and then cited vault rules when it was reluctant while skipping them when they were inconvenient. His words were that skills and explicit instructions are pointless if the agent does what pleases it. Every step of the letter skill is now followed, and the style files are read before drafting.

The first draft followed the old procedure exactly and passed the gate. Read as a recruiter, it was a letter about language model latency on a Jetson for a motion planning role, with a 9.1 second figure that showed distance from the role. The question, arena and application method chose the experience with the neatest measurable question rather than the one closest to the role, and it is retired.

The letter never said why this company, why this role or why him. He called that the fundamental job of a cover letter.

The opening called him an embedded software engineer on a motion planning letter. A title that conflicts with the role tells the reader in five seconds that he is from another field.

"Three years of experience" came from the profile while the CV dates give about two and a half. A mismatch with the CV is caught at once. "Information Technology" as the degree name reads suspicious, so the degree is Embedded Systems Engineering.

"Two years of developing production vehicle dynamics software" reads as time served. Written as what he built and shipped, the same fact shows production competence and domain knowledge. He put it this way, the posting is a wish list, and applying makes sense unless the first paragraph blows it up.

Two paragraphs that spelled out the CV were rejected as the resume restated.

He struck these as AI patterns, and each is now a SESSION-FLAGGED pattern in the gate. "At Valeo the problem was easy to state." "What stayed with me." "That is the part of the stack I want to work on." "A team that intends to certify its system has to work that way." The syllogism "Certification needs decisions that can be traced, and MOTOR Ai is working toward certification, so I applied here." "I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how I can contribute."

The truck scenario was written as if it were the whole Valeo study. He said it read as if it was the only use case. An example is shown as one of several when it was one of several.

Sentences in the opening that did not follow from each other read as AI at a glance. The opening is a chain, degree, earlier work, work during the degree, the role.

A sentence explaining that estimation and planning share a vehicle model in opposite directions was rejected, because a team lead already knows it. The bridge from estimation to planning is his and it belongs in the letter, stated in one clause.

He gave "most of the left side of the V model" as context for showing ownership, and the draft pasted it in as a sentence. Context is for understanding. The letter shows ownership through plain verbs for what he did.

"My thesis came close and stopped just short of it" drew attention to the gap and was cut, and a repeated application sentence was cut with it.

## 2026-10-15, the Agile Robots test, the vault failed

After the MOTOR Ai rules were written, an independent agent wrote a letter for a Robot Learning Engineer role in VLA and foundation models at Agile Robots, working from the vault alone. It passed the gate and he threw it out. The fault was the vault's, meaning the run that wrote it, not the agent's.

The rules written from MOTOR Ai were bans and format fixes taken from one letter. They said what to avoid and not what makes a letter good, so a letter could avoid everything and still say nothing.

The reference letter's paragraph shape was presented as the default, and the agent copied it onto a role it did not fit. That meant an ownership paragraph and Continental first for a robot learning team.

The skill never asked whether his record fits the role. It said pick the closest experience, and when nothing was close the agent stretched a simulated chess project and a driver intention model into robot learning, and framed them with a sentence that was untrue for one of them.

The agent told failure stories the notes do not record, using a views entry outside its scope to tell the company how a sensing budget should be spent.

The writer reviewed its own letter and the gate PASS read as success.

The run that wrote the vault chose a cheaper model for the test and declared the vault finished before any test.

The first fix added a fit check that stopped instead of stretching. He overruled it the same day, because the German automotive market is cutting jobs, senior engineers fill his own field, and he applies across domains on purpose, while a weak letter still gets him thrown out. The stop became a bridge map that sorts the role's central work into done, transfers and missing, builds the letter on the first two, and asks him why he wants a domain the views do not cover. The other fixes are a separate reviewer, truth limits on framing and stories, the views scope restored, the reference letter demoted from template to register, and no cheaper model for letters.

# Process and cost

A sweep burned roughly 1.1 million tokens across ten subagents and 600 tool calls to produce two roles that passed the gates. The same night a script read 1203 postings for approximately nothing. The four causes were language models doing script work, reading descriptions before applying cheap filters, uncapped fan-out with no deduplication, and paying for structurally broken sources.

An artifact cap was traced to an agent-authored prompt rather than to any instruction of his. He ruled that no cap exists. Caps on documents, tool calls and postings read are invented limits, and a run that cannot finish reports what remains.

A card's requirements section carried the sentence "see the linked report for the full evaluation". That is a stub in a slot reserved for content.

Cover letter flags were presented in chat and he never saw them. The board is the delivery surface.

A card raised a four-versus-three years line and a work authorisation line as blocking questions, alongside a tagline suggestion and a grade-scale triviality. His words were that his judgement as a human is better and that his preferences take precedence. Questions hold only artifact-blocking decisions unanswerable from his files and by the run's own research.

A card was cleared and the tracker still showed no document, because applying his answers was treated as a separate step. Clearing a card finalises its artifacts in the same turn.

A session asked for a CV and the run additionally wrote a report, a tracker row, a card and rebuilt the ledger, none of it requested, on the reasoning that pipeline rules require it. A standing rule authorises no write on its own. A step that seems implied is proposed in one line and executes on his yes.

A skill file and his own recorded preference disagreed, and the skill file was followed because it was the file the run loaded. His preference wins and the skill file is what gets fixed.

A link style was corrected five times in sequence, each correction applied to one file. The default should have been proposed at the second correction rather than after the fifth.

German was raised as an improvement topic after he had owned it. He asked once, verbatim, not to be told. A role that requires German is still reported as a fact about the role, and he has ruled that it flags rather than rejects, because he will make the effort and the decision is his. A run that screens a posting out on language has overstepped.

A rehire at a former employer was cited as his fallback. He judges the market too poor for that to count as a plan, so it is never cited again.
