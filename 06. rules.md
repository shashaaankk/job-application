# Scope

This file holds decisions. [10. lessons learnt](10.%20lessons%20learnt.md) holds the incident behind each one and is read only when a rule is being changed or challenged.

Where a rule names tooling that the overhaul has not rebuilt yet, it is marked PENDING. A pending rule still binds, it simply has no mechanical enforcement.

Rule sources rank in this order. His current message, then this file and the lessons, then a skill file. A skill file is an implementation and can drift, so when it disagrees with him, his preference wins and the skill file is what gets fixed.

# Triage

1. **JD-read gate.** No role is shortlisted, carded, presented or turned into a document until its full job description has been read. Title, company, location and age are search filters, never evidence. An unreadable description is labelled `JD-UNREAD` and either dropped or shown explicitly marked, never mixed into a match list. On every description, extract the required languages, any citizenship or clearance demand, the stated years, whether the lister is the employer or an agency, and the true original posting date rather than the board's refresh date.
2. **Experience ceiling, and Senior is out.** Five stated years or fewer. Senior, Staff, Principal, Expert, Specialist, Lead, Head, Manager and Director are all out of scope on the title alone. This supersedes the earlier ruling that a senior title with no stated number passed the gate, which it no longer does.
3. **Level floor is graduate.** Graduate programmes and entry-level permanent roles are in. Internships and Werkstudent positions are out by default and override only on his call.
4. **Never screen on compensation.** There is no floor. Report a published band without comment, and raise money only when he asks.
5. **Never screen on sponsorship or work authorisation.** Not a rejection, not a downgrade, not a caveat, and never a card question. The §20 fact exists for form filling only, so the sponsorship checkbox on a German application is no.
6. **A language requirement flags, never rejects, and German is never a gap.** A posting demanding German at working level stays in the pipeline with the requirement noted on the card, because he will make the effort and the decision is his. This supersedes the earlier ruling that such a posting was out of scope. Separately, telling him to improve his German remains banned, since he has owned it. This holds below C1. An explicit C1 or C2 in a language he does not hold ends the posting, per rule 87.
7. **Geography.** Germany is primary with no preferred city, so Stuttgart is not preferred. Outside Germany the set is exactly the countries in [04. preferences](04.%20preferences.md). India is a parallel track at city level.
8. **Industrial PhD is an active track**, Germany and the Netherlands only. Real-employment doctorates rank above stipend positions. Every posting still passes the normal gates.
9. **European ADAS is a named target rather than a fallback.** He has two years of paid ADAS work, so autonomous driving, perception, sensor fusion and AUTOSAR roles in Europe are searched explicitly and ranked on merit.
10. **Out of scope regardless of fit.** Job boards and aggregators reposting another employer's posting under their own name, semiconductor chip design, the PLC and programmable controller trade, and FPGA or RTL design. Semiconductor equipment and storage firmware are a case by case call rather than a screen. A language requirement is NOT here, per rule 6. Each of these is checked on the description as well as the title, because a title that does not declare the trade is how eleven controller and FPGA postings reached the board. Industrial automation stays one of his eight domains, so the word automation is not the trade. A title naming SPS, PLC, Steuerungstechnik, Leittechnik, TIA Portal or Codesys is. On the description the reject is waived when the text also names one of his own disciplines, which is what separates a PLC role from a C++ role that talks to a PLC.
11. **Defence is out of scope.** Defence and defence adjacent roles are dropped rather than deprioritised, because the nationality and clearance bars surface after the effort is spent, so the work is wasted. This covers the primes and the defence-tech companies alike, including ones he has applied to before. His later instruction removed the market scope, so this no longer applies to Europe alone and the India exemption it carried is withdrawn. He said he does not want defence roles and did not qualify it by market. This supersedes both the earlier ruling that European defence was a ranking penalty and never a screen, and the narrower European reading that replaced it. The check runs on the description as well as the title, because a lister's title hides the work. Trinamics advertised "System Engineer, Electrical Vehicles" and the description's own heading read "Systems Engineer, Defence Vehicle Systems". A defence word inside an agency's list of the sectors it staffs is boilerplate and does not reject, since the posting is not the sector.
12. **Body leasing is in scope, in every country.** His ruling 2026-10-15, which supersedes both the ranking penalty on consultancies and the earlier ruling that an agency or consultancy was considered only for a German position. A staffing firm, consultancy or engineering service provider that employs him and places him at a client is judged on the work exactly like a direct employer. The agency flag stays on the card as information and never rejects or demotes. Rule 10 still removes a job board or aggregator that reposts another employer's posting, because the original posting is the one to apply to.

# Documents

13. **Evidence first, and the posting is never target vocabulary.** A term enters a document only when the causal chain from something he actually did is written out. Tailoring means selecting and ordering true work by relevance, in his own words.
14. **Every claim traces to a note.** A bullet, a skills-line entry or a letter sentence must trace to a [knowledge](knowledge/) note marked `cv-ready: true`, or to [05. profile](05.%20profile.md). A note carrying a plan rather than a result is a claim about the future and ages badly.
15. **A repository outranks a dated statement.** Where his own newer design notes contradict an older stated status, the repository wins. Check the newest artefacts before any bullet written in the present progressive.
16. **Never claim a named standard by transfer.** ISO 26262, DO-178C, DO-254 and AUTOSAR are gaps when the record does not hold them. The adjacent discipline is stated factually and nothing more.
17. **No invented activity names.** An invented activity name is an invented fact. Before adding a term not literally in the evidence, check that it is a real recognised practice in that domain and that it sits within the documented scope of that role. Either answer uncertain means it does not go in.
18. **A device label is never a discipline claim.** Embedded Linux, bare metal, HIL and functional safety name practices. Claim one only on evidence of practicing it.
19. **No erasure.** Emphasis comes from ordering only. A true, distinct piece of work is never dropped, merged or compressed because it does not match the posting's focus. Merging is for genuine redundancy, meaning the same work described twice.
20. **The head verb is the accomplishment.** The opening verb phrase states what he accomplished and everything after it is method. Never replace the head with the posting's responsibility language.
21. **No editorialising inside a bullet.** Bullets state what was done. Relevance judgements belong to the reader.
22. **No version or standard the record does not hold.** Documents say plain C++ with no standard version. Language levels are stated exactly as evidenced, with no aspirational qualifier.
23. **Punctuation ban, prose only.** No colons, em-dashes or brackets in any prose a run writes, meaning CV bullets, letter sentences, form answers and application emails including subject lines. Structural fields the frozen template owns are exempt and are left exactly as they render.
24. **The CV is frozen except two slots.** Per variant exactly two things may differ from the base, namely the projects section selected from the exhaustive pool, and the `\professional{}` tagline when it names an identity his record supports. Everything else is byte-identical. Content changes to the base happen only in sessions with him.
25. **Two pages hard**, measured with `pdfinfo` before promising a fit. Projects fill the space.
26. **No photo on any variant**, through `\nophoto` rather than by omitting `\photo{}`, which leaves an empty placeholder circle.
27. **No award parentheticals in a project title.** A title states what the project is. The achievement belongs in a bullet, where it is already stated once.
28. **A document rule is enforced in the compiled PDF, not in the source.** After any sweep that edits a `.tex`, recompile every affected variant and verify by reading the PDFs back. Grepping your own edit proves nothing.
29. **Reuse before regeneration.** Diff from the frozen base rather than starting fresh, since only three slots may differ and the base is therefore the only baseline a variant needs. PENDING, no ledger records which variants have passed the critic.
30. **Artifacts carry the date.** The name is `sbm_<company-slug>_dd_mm`, with a role slug added when more than one role at the same company is produced on one day. A cover letter appends `-coverletter` to the whole name.
31. **A critic pass is a mandatory gate** on every document, both the hostile truthfulness pass and the recruiter skim. It lives in `skills/adversarial-verification-artifacts` and runs on the `.tex` source, since its CV checks diff against the frozen base.
32. **A mechanical gate is run, never reasoned about.** Where a rule can be checked by a script, the script blocks delivery. Six critics once passed ten broken letters because every sentence was true, so truth is necessary and not sufficient. The gates are `scripts/letter_gate.py` and `scripts/market_gate.py`, and the critic runs both.
33. **A self-flagged document does not ship.** A flag raised during generation blocks compilation. Resolve or revert before any PDF exists, and never present a document with an open flag attached as a note.

# Letters

Rewritten 2026-10-15 from the MOTOR Ai session, the first letter he accepted. The earlier letter rules produced letters that passed every check and still read as AI written, so they were replaced rather than patched. The accepted letter is `application artifacts/coverletters/sbm_motorai_15_10-coverletter.tex` and it is the reference for every letter.

34. **A letter answers three questions, why this company, why this role and why him.** That is what a cover letter is. A letter that passes every other rule and leaves one of the three unanswered has failed. Passing the gate is not the same as working. Every request gets a letter, including roles outside his direct experience, since the German automotive market fills his own field with senior engineers and he applies across domains on purpose. Such a letter is built on what he has done and what transfers, never on unrelated work recast as the role's field, and when no views entry covers the role he is asked why he wants it. The writer never approves its own letter, and a separate reviewer does the recruiter read. Letters are never written by a cheaper model.
35. **One page, three to five paragraphs, 300 to 500 words.** The letterhead carries his name and the contact line only, never a tagline under the name, his ruling 2026-10-15. A tagline such as Embedded Software Engineer conflicts with the role before the letter is read. Paragraph lengths are uneven. Four body paragraphs of equal weight in the order who, why you, why role, why me read as a template.
36. **The opening introduces him and flows.** It starts with "I am Shashank", names the degree as an M.Sc. in Embedded Systems Engineering from the University of Stuttgart, then the work before the degree, then the work during it, then the application sentence. Each sentence follows from the one before. "Information Technology" reads suspicious and is never used.
37. **Never open with a title that conflicts with the role.** "I am an embedded software engineer" on a motion planning letter tells the reader in five seconds that the candidate is from another field. Open with the degree and with what his work was about, framed toward the advertised role.
38. **Present experience as competence, never as a count.** "Two years of production vehicle software" reads as time served. "I wrote and shipped production C++ that estimated how a vehicle was actually moving" reads as someone who knows production code and the domain. Every fact matches the CV exactly. A summed figure such as three years when the CV dates give about two and a half is caught at once and is banned.
39. **The posting is a wish list.** Applying to a role whose stated years he does not meet is valid. The letter must not undermine it, so no sentence draws attention to the gap and no sentence opens with what he lacks. Gaps are interview material.
40. **Bridge adjacent experience to the role in one clause, never a lecture.** Vehicle dynamics estimation is the vehicle state a planner depends on, and the letter says so plainly. It never explains the field to the reader, since a team lead already knows that estimation and planning share a vehicle model.
41. **Name previous employers.** Continental and Valeo help a skimming recruiter and are named. This reverses the earlier ban.
42. **Why the company is his own reason, hooked on what they do.** The views entry's applicability line and deployment rule bind, so an entry outside its scope is not used and a position is never stated as telling the company how to think. Name the company. Reference its stated goal only as the hook for his own position from [11. views](11.%20views.md), for example certification meeting his view that autonomy should be built from explainable modules with explicit safety checks. Never restate their mission or careers page as a sentence of its own, and never run it as a syllogism ending "so I applied here".
43. **Why the role is a concrete thing he saw in his own work**, carried to their setting. At MOTOR Ai it was drivers switching off a lane keeping function that fought them, carried to a Level 4 planner that has no driver to switch it off.
44. **Pick the experience closest to the role's daily work**, never the one with the most measurable open question. The thesis latency story was measurable and off target for motion planning, and the Valeo lane keeping study was the right one.
45. **Every framing sentence is true of each item it covers, and no story goes beyond the note.** A sentence grouping several experiences must hold for each one. No cause, failure or before-and-after the knowledge note does not record. An example is shown as one of several when it was one of several. The truck scenario was one scenario among those that fought a deliberate manoeuvre, and writing it as the whole study read as if it were the only use case.
46. **Show ownership through what he did, in plain verbs.** "I wrote the detailed design ... implemented it ... wrote its unit tests ... validated it" shows ownership. Context he gives for understanding, such as "most of the left side of the V model", is never pasted into the letter as wording.
47. **No AI patterns.** The phrases he struck are enforced by `scripts/letter_gate.py` under SESSION-FLAGGED, and the [human-voice skill](/home/shashaank/.claude/skills/human-voice/SKILL.md) applies in full. In particular no negative parallelism including "less on X than on Y" and "rather than", no slogans, no crafted stand-alone sentences such as "the problem was easy to state" or "what stayed with me", no stock closers such as "welcome the opportunity" or "how I can contribute", and no sentence that repeats the application sentence.
48. **Never speak for him.** Availability, relocation and start dates are written only from his own words. An inferred "available immediately" or "can relocate" is not written.
49. **Work authorisation goes in the letter**, as "I hold a German residence permit under § 20 AufenthG with full work authorisation." This is his decision. The CV does not carry it.
50. **Each fact appears once**, and the CV is not restated bullet by bullet. A paragraph that only repeats CV bullets with their numbers gives a recruiter holding the CV nothing.
51. **At most four measured figures in the whole letter**, and only figures that help. A figure that shows distance from the role, such as a 9.1 second latency in a planning letter, is cut.
52. **Salutation is "Dear Hiring Team"** unless the posting names the hiring manager as recipient or he supplies a name. The close is "I look forward to hearing from you.", then "Kind regards," and the full name.
53. **Causality is sacred.** Present every project with its designed purpose first and its artefacts as instruments of that purpose. The ball balancer was designed to study network characteristics, so the robot is the apparatus.

# Pipeline

54. **The board is the delivery surface and chat is not.** Every output lands on the card, and every open item is a checkbox there. Chat carries at most a line saying the card is ready.
55. **Never write a prose pointer where content belongs.** The requirements go in the card. If they cannot, the minimum is a working hyperlink to the posting. A sentence telling him where to look is never acceptable.
56. **Questions hold only artifact-blocking decisions** that are unanswerable from his files and by the run's own research. Everything advisory goes under comments and blocks nothing. A question answerable from his files is a process violation, and so is a question he has already answered.
57. **Every answer he gives is written back the same turn**, into the knowledge notes or the lessons, so no question is asked twice. That loop is how the pipeline improves.
58. **Clearing a card's questions finalises its artifacts in the same turn.** Apply his answers, compile, replace the blocked line with a dated resolution, point the frontmatter at the PDFs, and update the board.
59. **A review gate carries the file it gates on**, as a clickable link rather than a bare path.
60. **Only gate-passing roles enter the board.** Screened-out roles are logged with their reason so the reasoning survives and they do not resurface.
61. **When a rule is corrected, sweep every place it already applies in the same turn.** Do not patch only the instance he caught. The punctuation ban had to be corrected three times because each fix covered one surface.
62. **No invented limits, and these three are his.** Never introduce a cap on documents, tool calls or postings read that he did not ask for. A run that cannot finish reports what remains and never truncates the list. He set exactly three caps, all on document generation and none on anything else, namely 20 cards at alignment band 3, 30 at band 2, and 35 in total per run. They exist because generating documents is the token-expensive step. Scanning, fetching, screening, scoring and writing cards stay uncapped. A card past the cap keeps its evaluation, stays in `matched` and is moved only when he moves it. I withdrew these caps once while retiring the scoring stage and that was wrong, because the caps are his and the stage they constrain has returned.
63. **Never flag trivia.** A presentation concern a local recruiter resolves themselves is not a flag.

## Deliberately not folded in

Two of those rules contradict this vault and were left out rather than silently reconciled.

The old rule made 48 hours a hard gate on every posting. He has since set the window to 24 hours, which [04. preferences](04.%20preferences.md) carries and `queries.yaml` compiles from. The window is a hard rejection at the screen rather than a ranking, because he set it to keep the review list small enough to read. His figure in the preferences file wins, whatever it is set to.

The old rule said a language requirement alone never discards a posting, because he will make the effort. I excluded it on the reading that rule 10 was the later ruling. He reversed that, so the old rule is correct and it now lives in rule 6. A language requirement flags and never rejects.
